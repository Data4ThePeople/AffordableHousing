## Description

```
A 57-second tour of Rentals Within Reach, a free interactive map of every U.S. county showing the share of rental homes a household can afford at 30% of its income.

Try it here: https://www.data4thepeople.com/p/rentals-within-reach

In this video:
1. Change the household income: the median renter, the median of all households, or an income percentile
2. Zoom to one state (here, Florida)
3. Press Play to step through four periods, 2005-2009 to 2020-2024
4. Click a county to see its math, its rentals by monthly rent, and how its rentals changed
5. Pick a county from the rankings
6. Switch between fewest and most within reach
7. Reset, then search any county by name

Each bubble is a county, sized by its number of rentals. The color is the share of those rentals priced at or under what the household can pay. You can also enter your own income, count income before or after taxes, change the share of income spent on rent, and leave out college counties.

For the median renter household, spending 30% of after-tax income, about 31% of rentals are within reach nationally in 2020-2024. For the median of all households it is about 66%.

Data: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 5-year estimates for 2005-2009, 2010-2014, 2015-2019 and 2020-2024 (gross rent and household income). After-tax income is modeled with NBER TAXSIM and Social Security Administration payroll tax rates. Rentals are occupied units, counted by what the current tenant pays, so this is not a count of homes on the market.

Built by Data 4 The People. Free to use, no signup.
https://www.data4thepeople.com
```
